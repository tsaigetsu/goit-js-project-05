iAmDeveloper — Personal Portfolio Website

What this site is about
This is a personal portfolio website for a full-stack developer, Lloyd Jefferson. The site introduces the developer, highlights technical skills, showcases projects, and provides a way for potential clients or employers to get in touch. Its primary goal is to present professionalism and credibility through a clean and structured online presence.

What the user gets
A quick introduction to the developer’s background and expertise.
A clear overview of technical skills and areas of specialization.
Insight into previous projects and work examples.
An understanding of the developer’s approach and work values.
Easy access to contact information and social media for collaboration.

Key strengths
Clean design and structure — easy to navigate and read.
Clear personal branding — communicates professionalism and identity.
Transparency — skills, experience, and contacts are presented openly.
User-friendly navigation — sections are logically ordered and accessible.
